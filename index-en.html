Philosophy

“Don't run. Soon everything will end”

A modern person lives in a state of constant information noise. Arriving at a museum, theater, or library, they bring this noise with them. Their attention is scattered, their brain is overloaded.

Neurotunnel "The State Tunnel" is designed in the style of Emotech (Emotional Tech) — emotionally-active architecture that reacts to a person's state. Combining biomorphic structures or the geometry of a rectangular prism by Alena Levitskaya (AB "STRUCTURE") and physical algorithms by Valery Latypov, we have created an adaptive filter.

The space reads the biometrics of the entering person and guides them through a metaphorical "life cycle" — from the sensory silence of "birth", through the light-acoustic experience of interacting with the space, to "symbolic death" (a visual reset with white light) and final rebirth in a state of pure balance and peace.

Unified Ecosystem: Bridge & GES-2

The bridge near GES-2 works as an emotional enticing portal that seamlessly flows into a large-scale indoor exhibition.

1. Bridge as a City Portal

An entry point and first filter of attention. Crossing the river, the visitor is drawn into a play of light, sensors smoothly read biometrics, preparing them for deep immersion. This is a showcase of the exhibition, visible from all embankments.

2. Exhibition on GES-2 Level -1

Primary space. The dark underground halls of GES-2 represent the ideal location for the installation. The visitor descends into silence and semi-darkness, where they pass through a 20-meter individual state tunnel, finishing the journey in the room of catharsis.

3. Alternative: Central Nave

Monumental dominant. Placing a massive parametric tunnel in the main avenue (atrium) of GES-2. The giant object made of white biomorphic ribs becomes the central sculpture, reacting to the collective state of people in the building.

Uniqueness and Innovation

Why are there no analogues in the world?

Existing light and neuro-installations (such as Nick Verstand's 'Aura' or Ivo Peitzner's 'Tunnel of Sentient Light') operate purely as passive mirrors — they only read and visualize the user's current biorhythms and stress.

1. Active Compensation (Bioregulation)

The Neurotunnel operates as an active balancer. Algorithms do not just show your anxiety, but create a compensating effect: if you are stressed, the system selects calming sound frequencies (theta rhythms 4–7 Hz) and cold blue light. For apathy — an energizing gold spectrum and beta rhythms.

2. Fully Contactless

Instead of uncomfortable EEG helmets or physical sensors, data reading happens on the go. Cameras track the micro-pulsations of facial vessels (rPPG) and facial expressions (Face Mesh) without hindering the person's movement.

3. Individual Light in Group Passage

During group passages, the system projects individual light contours around each visitor, which synchronize with their personal frequency. When meeting or approaching, aura colors smoothly blend, avoiding cacophony and creating a visual dialogue.

4. Privacy (Privacy by Design)

All biometric metrics are processed solely in RAM in real time. Personal data, photos, or video recordings of faces are not saved or transmitted to external networks.

How does the contactless sensor work?

rPPG Technology

Contactless pulse: The camera captures subtle changes in facial skin color caused by blood flow in vessels with each heartbeat. Based on this, HR and heart rate variability (HRV) are calculated, estimating stress levels.

CV Emotion Mesh

Neural network facial analysis: Computer vision models analyze 468 facial landmark points in real time, reading micro-tension in the forehead, eyes, and mouth muscles, determining up to 8 basic emotional states.

Acoustic Gateway

Super-directional sound: Sound frequencies of balance are broadcast through special acoustic lenses (parametric speakers), localizing the therapeutic effect strictly above the person passing.

Technical Specifications (Specs)

Hardware

  • Structure: Modular frame made of lightweight alloy (quick assembly).
  • Screens: Seamless flexible LED panels (1.8 - 2.5 mm pitch) on walls, ceiling, and floor.
  • Sensors: LiDAR for tracking, IR camera for contactless pulse, facial expression sensor.
  • Acoustics: Directional sound array (Audio Spotlight) for audio field isolation.

Software

  • Neuroanalysis: Real-time psychoemotional index calculation.
  • Generative Engine: Dynamic light rendering via TouchDesigner / Unreal Engine.
  • Binaural Synthesis: Real-time generation of compensating audio frequencies.
  • Export: Automatic rendering of 15-second video report with QR code cloud upload.
